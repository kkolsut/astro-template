\Abstract{%
Text of the abstract. State the principal conclusion of the work plainly,
including the measured value with its uncertainty and the confidence level
of any limit.
}{%
key words -- from the common list -- separated by commas%

%% ----------------------------------------------------------------------
\section{Introduction}

What the question is, what is already known, and what this paper adds.
In-text citation in Acta is author--year in running text: Udalski
\textit{et al.\ }(2015) found\dots, or parenthetically (Udalski
\textit{et al.\ }2015). Every citation here must have a matching
\verb|\refitem| in the reference list below.

\section{Observations and Data}

Instrument, dates, filters or setup, exposure times, and the archive query
or program ID the data came from. Give the time system explicitly
(BJD\_TDB unless stated otherwise) and say which reduction scripts under
\texttt{scripts/} produced each product.

\section{Analysis}

\subsection{Subsection Title}

Method, with the assumptions stated. Equations are numbered and referred
to as Eq.~(\ref{eq:example}):

\begin{equation}
P = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{a^{3}}{GM}}\,.
\label{eq:example}
\end{equation}
